The Victim

Richard Blackwood — 52

A wealthy businessman who lived alone in Blackwood Mansion.
He had recently announced that he was changing his will.
That announcement gave several people a reason to want him dead.
The Crime Scene
Richard was discovered in his study at 11:47 PM.

Details:
Study door was locked.
Window was open.
A glass of wine was found on the desk.
His phone was missing.
A broken clock was found on the floor.
No weapon was found.
